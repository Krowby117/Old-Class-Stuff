# Discussion 01 - "The Dream Team"
### Readings from "Peopleware: Productive Projects and Teams"
- Ch. 21 : The Whole is Greater Than the Sum (5 pages) 
- Ch. 23 : Teamicide (7 pages) 
- Ch. 17 : Playing Well With Others (3 pages)

### Reading 1 - Chapter 21:
- The team is often in it for the love of the game.

- A ***jelled team*** is a group of people so strongly knit that the whole is greater than the sum of its parts

- Teams are formed around goals and common goals help teams jell. 
Team members might each have their own personal goals they bring into the project, but there must be
a central, common goal between them. Even if it's as simple as delivering a solid, well built product

- Important to understand what a goal means to you and what it means to your teammates

- A delegation came to talk to me. The company’s chances for the best second quarter in 
history were in our hands, they said. They asked me to share this fact with the rest 
of the team, “to focus their efforts." The energy went out of the team like wind 
out of a sail. The chief programmer summed it all up: “Who gives a rat’s ass for their 
second quarter?” Half an hour later, they’d all gone home.

- Team members are more than just what they bring to the team. 
    - "... that the fellow you know as a  database  specialist  is  more  inclined
        to  describe  himself  as  a  father,  a  Boy Scout leader, 
        and a member of the local school board."

### Reading 2 -  Chapter 23:
- You can't force a team to jell and work together well

- Stuck? Start coming up with ways to not achieve your goals
    - Helps declutter your brain
    - Gives things that should be avoided while trying to achieve your goal

- Teamicide techniques:
    - ***Defensive Management*** : Trust your team and let them do the work. "If the staff comes to 
                                believe it’s not allowed to make any errors of its own, 
                                the message that you don’t trust them comes through loud 
                                and clear. There is no message you can send that will better 
                                inhibit team formation.
    - ***Physical Separation*** : Keeps the teams from forming connections and being able
                            to effectively work together on tasks
    - ***Time Fragmentation*** : Forcing people into multiple teams and projects is bad.
                            They lose connections with people, teams, and the work
                            they're doing.
    - ***Phony Deadlines*** : Nobody likes being rushed or having to deal with Deadlines
                        that are arbitrary and impossible to meet. Nobody likes
                        crunch time or to be rushed.

### Reading 3 - Chapter 17:
- Diversity in teams is needed and beneficial

- "Woman changed the way that teams were organized and how team members interacted. 
They complemented our tired old sports analogies with new images from ballet, 
child rearing, and family dynamics. Today, an all-male team seems thin and 
less than totally energized. Women made a huge difference."

- "Just  as  we  relish  the  different  foods  diverse  members  may  bring  to  the table, 
so too we need to relish the different ways they tend to work and think and communicate."

- Different ideas and experiences can help a team grow together and create an even better product 
in the end.

- New stuff is good for people but too much new stuff is terrible for teams.
